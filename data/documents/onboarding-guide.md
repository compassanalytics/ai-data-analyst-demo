# Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to Our Platform

This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started with our platform. Whether you're a solo user or setting up your entire organization, we've designed the onboarding process to be straightforward and efficient.

## Quick Start Checklist

- [ ] Create your account
- [ ] Verify your email
- [ ] Complete your profile
- [ ] Invite team members
- [ ] Connect your first data source
- [ ] Complete the interactive tutorial
- [ ] Set up your first dashboard

## Step 1: Account Setup

### Creating Your Organization Account

1. Visit our signup page at signup.company.com
2. Enter your work email address
3. Choose your organization name (this will be your subdomain: yourorg.company.com)
4. Set a secure password (minimum 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols)
5. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
6. Click "Create Account"

### Email Verification

After signup, you'll receive a verification email. Click the link to verify your account. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes:
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Whitelist emails from @company.com
- Contact support for assistance

## Step 2: Team Invitation

### Inviting Team Members

You can invite team members in several ways:

**Email Invitation**
1. Navigate to Settings > Team Management
2. Click "Invite Members"
3. Enter email addresses (comma-separated for bulk invites)
4. Select their default role
5. Click "Send Invitations"

**SSO Directory Sync**
For organizations using SSO:
1. Navigate to Settings > Identity Provider
2. Connect your IdP (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace)
3. Configure user provisioning (SCIM)
4. Users will be automatically provisioned on first login

### Role Assignment

Assign appropriate roles based on responsibilities:
- **Admin**: For IT administrators and platform managers
- **Manager**: For team leads who need reporting access
- **User**: For standard team members
- **Viewer**: For stakeholders who need read-only access

## Step 3: Data Source Integration

### Supported Data Sources

We support connections to:

**Databases**
- PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle
- Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift
- MongoDB, Cassandra

**Cloud Services**
- Salesforce, HubSpot
- Google Analytics, Mixpanel
- Stripe, QuickBooks
- AWS S3, Azure Blob Storage

**Files**
- CSV, Excel, JSON
- Parquet, Avro

### Connecting Your First Data Source

1. Navigate to Data > Connections
2. Click "Add Connection"
3. Select your data source type
4. Enter connection credentials
5. Test the connection
6. Configure sync schedule (real-time or scheduled)
7. Save the connection

## Step 4: Interactive Tutorial

Our 30-minute interactive tutorial covers:

1. **Platform Overview** (5 min): Navigation and key features
2. **Data Exploration** (10 min): Querying and analyzing your data
3. **Dashboard Creation** (10 min): Building your first visualization
4. **Collaboration** (5 min): Sharing and working with your team

Access the tutorial from your dashboard or navigate to Help > Interactive Tutorial.

## Step 5: Going Live

Once you've completed the setup:

1. **Review Settings**: Ensure all configurations are correct
2. **Test Workflows**: Verify data connections and permissions
3. **Train Your Team**: Share the tutorial link with team members
4. **Set Up Alerts**: Configure notifications for important events
5. **Go Live**: Start using the platform in production
