# Velocity Motors — Employee Compensation & Commission Structure

This document defines the compensation framework, commission calculations, sales quotas, territory assignments, and F&I manager incentives for all Velocity Motors sales personnel. For details on F&I products referenced in commission calculations, see [Financing & Payment Terms](financing-terms.md).

## Sales Force Overview

Velocity Motors employs approximately **50 salespersons** across all dealership locations. The top **10%** of salespersons (approximately **5 individuals**) hold **Sales Manager** positions, overseeing teams, approving deals, and handling escalated negotiations.

### Role Distribution

| Role | Count | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Manager | ~5 | Team oversight, deal approval, escalation handling, quota setting |
| Senior Salesperson | ~10 | Full sales cycle, mentoring, assigned key accounts |
| Salesperson | ~25 | Full sales cycle, walk-in and internet leads |
| Junior Salesperson | ~10 | Supervised sales, lead qualification, test drives |

## Commission Formula

All salespersons earn commission on each completed vehicle sale. The commission rate is calculated using the following formula:

**Commission Rate = Base Rate (1.5%) + (Seniority Factor x 2.5%)**

Where:

- **Base Rate**: 1.5% of the gross profit on each sale (applies to all salespersons regardless of tenure)
- **Seniority Factor**: Calculated as `min(years_of_service / 10, 1.0)` — this means the seniority factor scales linearly from 0.0 to 1.0 over the first 10 years of service, capping at 1.0 for employees with 10 or more years
- **Gross Profit**: The difference between the sale price and the dealership's cost basis (invoice + reconditioning + transportation)

### Commission Rate by Tenure

The following table shows the effective commission rate at each career stage:

| Career Stage | Years of Service | Seniority Factor | Commission Rate | Example on $5,000 Gross Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New Hire** | 0 - 1 year | 0.00 - 0.10 | 1.5% - 1.75% | $75 - $88 |
| **Early Career** | 1 - 3 years | 0.10 - 0.30 | 2.0% - 2.25% | $100 - $113 |
| **Mid Career** | 3 - 5 years | 0.30 - 0.50 | 2.25% - 2.75% | $113 - $138 |
| **Senior** | 5 - 10 years | 0.50 - 1.00 | 2.75% - 4.0% | $138 - $200 |
| **Veteran** | 10+ years | 1.00 (capped) | 4.0% | $200 |

### Commission Calculation Examples

**Example 1: New Hire (6 months)**
- Seniority Factor: 0.5 / 10 = 0.05
- Commission Rate: 1.5% + (0.05 x 2.5%) = 1.625%
- Sale Gross Profit: $4,200
- Commission: $4,200 x 1.625% = **$68.25**

**Example 2: Mid Career (4 years)**
- Seniority Factor: 4 / 10 = 0.40
- Commission Rate: 1.5% + (0.40 x 2.5%) = 2.5%
- Sale Gross Profit: $6,000
- Commission: $6,000 x 2.5% = **$150.00**

**Example 3: Veteran (12 years)**
- Seniority Factor: min(12 / 10, 1.0) = 1.0
- Commission Rate: 1.5% + (1.0 x 2.5%) = 4.0%
- Sale Gross Profit: $8,500
- Commission: $8,500 x 4.0% = **$340.00**

## Sales Quotas

Every salesperson is assigned a monthly sales quota denominated in **total gross profit dollars**. Quota levels are determined by tenure and career stage using the following formula:

**Monthly Quota = Base Quota x (1 + Seniority Factor x 0.5)**

Where:

- **Base Quota**: Ranges from $80,000 to $150,000 depending on the salesperson's assigned territory and historical territory performance
- **Seniority Factor**: Same calculation as commission: `min(years_of_service / 10, 1.0)`

### Quota Ranges by Career Stage

| Career Stage | Base Quota Range | Effective Quota Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New Hire** (0-1 yr) | $80,000 - $100,000 | $80,000 - $105,000 | Reduced quota during ramp-up period |
| **Early Career** (1-3 yr) | $90,000 - $120,000 | $95,000 - $138,000 | Standard territory assignment |
| **Mid Career** (3-5 yr) | $100,000 - $130,000 | $115,000 - $163,000 | May manage key accounts |
| **Senior** (5-10 yr) | $110,000 - $150,000 | $138,000 - $225,000 | Includes key account and fleet quota |
| **Veteran** (10+ yr) | $120,000 - $150,000 | $180,000 - $225,000 | Highest quota, highest commission rate |

### Quota Attainment Bonuses

In addition to per-sale commissions, salespersons earn bonuses for quota attainment:

| Attainment Level | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 100% of quota | $500 monthly bonus |
| 110% of quota | $1,000 monthly bonus |
| 120% of quota | $2,000 monthly bonus |
| 150%+ of quota | $5,000 monthly bonus + President's Club eligibility |

Quota attainment is measured monthly. Consecutive quarterly attainment above 110% qualifies the salesperson for promotion consideration.

### Territory Assignment

Each of the **18 territories** is assigned 2-3 salespersons based on territory volume. Territory assignments consider:

- **Seniority**: Senior salespersons receive first choice during annual territory realignment
- **Specialization**: Salespersons with luxury brand expertise (BMW, Mercedes-Benz) are preferentially assigned to territories with higher luxury demand
- **Performance**: Underperforming territories may be reassigned mid-year at the Regional Sales Manager's discretion
- **Balance**: No territory should have more than 15% revenue variance from the territory average within its region

## Sales Manager Compensation

Sales Managers (top 10%, approximately 5 individuals) receive a modified compensation structure:

### Manager Base Salary

Sales Managers receive a base salary of **$60,000 - $85,000** annually (salespersons do not receive a base salary — they are commission-only with a draw).

### Manager Override Commission

In addition to personal sales commissions, Sales Managers earn an **override commission** of 0.5% on all gross profit generated by their team. For a team of 8-10 salespersons generating $800,000-$1,200,000 in monthly gross profit, this translates to $4,000-$6,000 per month in override income.

### Manager Bonus Structure

- **Team Quota Attainment**: $1,500 bonus when the team achieves 100% of aggregate quota
- **Turnover Reduction**: $2,500 quarterly bonus if team voluntary turnover is below 10% annualized
- **CSI Score**: $1,000 monthly bonus if team Customer Satisfaction Index averages 4.5+ stars

## F&I Manager Compensation

The **Finance and Insurance (F&I) Manager** handles all financing, leasing, and aftermarket product sales. F&I Managers earn compensation through per-deal fees and penetration bonuses.

### Per-Deal F&I Income

The F&I Manager earns a flat fee plus a percentage of the profit on each F&I product sold:

| F&I Product | Customer Cost | F&I Manager Per-Deal Fee | Typical Dealer Profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extended Warranty (3-Year) | $1,500 - $2,500 | $75 - $125 per contract | $400 - $700 |
| Extended Warranty (5-Year) | $2,500 - $4,000 | $100 - $175 per contract | $600 - $1,100 |
| Gap Insurance | $400 - $800 | $50 - $100 per policy | $200 - $450 |

### Penetration Bonuses

F&I Managers earn quarterly bonuses based on **product penetration rates** (percentage of eligible deals where the product is sold):

| Product | Target Penetration | Quarterly Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Extended Warranty (any plan) | 45% | $2,000 |
| Gap Insurance | 55% | $1,500 |
| Both Products on Same Deal | 35% | $3,000 additional |

### F&I Income Benchmarks

A high-performing F&I Manager targets **$1,200 - $1,800** in total F&I income per retail deal (combining Extended Warranty, Gap Insurance, and finance reserve). At 80-120 retail deals per month across the dealership, total F&I department gross ranges from $96,000 to $216,000 monthly.

## Compensation Administration

### Pay Schedule

- **Commissions**: Paid semi-monthly on the 15th and last day of each month for deals funded in the prior pay period
- **Base Salary** (Managers): Paid bi-weekly
- **Bonuses**: Paid monthly (quota bonuses) or quarterly (penetration bonuses, special incentives)

### Commission Draw

Junior salespersons and new hires receive a **recoverable draw** of $2,000 per month for the first 6 months. The draw is an advance against future commissions. If commissions earned exceed the draw, the salesperson keeps the excess. If commissions are below the draw, the deficit accumulates and is recovered from future commission earnings.

### Clawback Provisions

Commissions are subject to clawback if the associated deal is unwound within **90 days** due to financing rejection, customer exchange (see [Return & Exchange Policy](return-exchange-policy.md)), or fraud. The full commission amount is deducted from the salesperson's next pay period.
