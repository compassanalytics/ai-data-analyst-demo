# Velocity Motors — Financing & Payment Terms

This document defines all financing options, payment methods, customer segmentation, credit policies, and insurance products available through Velocity Motors. For warranty add-on pricing, see [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md). For exchange-related financing adjustments, see [Return & Exchange Policy](return-exchange-policy.md).

## Payment Methods

Velocity Motors accepts **4 payment methods** for vehicle purchases. The distribution across all transactions is as follows:

| Payment Method | Share of Transactions | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Financing** | 55% | Loan through Velocity Motors lending partners |
| **Cash** | 20% | Full payment at time of purchase (check, wire, certified funds) |
| **Lease** | 15% | Closed-end lease through Velocity Motors leasing partners |
| **Trade-In + Financing** | 10% | Trade-in vehicle equity applied as down payment, balance financed |

### Financing (55% of Transactions)

Velocity Motors partners with a network of lenders to offer competitive financing. Loan approval is based on credit score, income verification, and debt-to-income ratio. The dealership submits applications to multiple lenders simultaneously to secure the best rate for the customer.

### Cash Purchases (20% of Transactions)

Cash purchases require full payment before delivery. Accepted forms: cashier's check, certified check, wire transfer, or ACH transfer. Personal checks are accepted with a 10-business-day hold before delivery. Cash buyers are still encouraged to purchase Extended Warranty and Gap Insurance products.

### Lease (15% of Transactions)

Leases are available on **New vehicles only**. CPO and Used vehicles are not eligible for leasing. Lease terms, mileage allowances, and residual values are set by the manufacturer's leasing division. End-of-lease options include return, purchase at residual value, or lease a new vehicle.

### Trade-In + Financing (10% of Transactions)

Trade-in equity is applied as a down payment, reducing the financed amount. Trade-in values are determined using current market data and a physical inspection. Negative equity (owing more than the trade-in value) can be rolled into the new loan, subject to lender approval and loan-to-value limits.

## Customer Segments

Velocity Motors classifies all customers into **3 segments**, each with distinct credit parameters and payment terms:

### Individual Customers (SEG-001)

- **Share of Customers**: 70%
- **Credit Score Range**: 580 - 850
- **Credit Limit**: Up to $50,000
- **Payment Terms**: Net 30 days
- **Eligibility**: All payment methods (Financing, Cash, Lease, Trade-In + Financing)
- **Documentation Required**: Valid driver's license, proof of income (2 most recent pay stubs or tax return), proof of residence, proof of insurance

### Fleet Customers (SEG-002)

- **Share of Customers**: 20%
- **Credit Score Range**: 650 - 850
- **Credit Limit**: Up to $500,000
- **Payment Terms**: Net 45 days
- **Eligibility**: Financing, Cash, Trade-In + Financing (leasing available through separate fleet lease program)
- **Documentation Required**: Business tax ID, business financial statements (2 years), authorized buyer letter, fleet insurance certificate
- **Additional Benefits**: Priority scheduling for service (see [Service & Maintenance Policy](service-maintenance-policy.md)), quarterly inspection program, volume pricing, dedicated account manager

### Dealer Customers (SEG-003)

- **Share of Customers**: 10%
- **Credit Score Range**: 700 - 850
- **Credit Limit**: Up to $1,000,000
- **Payment Terms**: Net 60 days
- **Eligibility**: Financing (floor plan), Cash
- **Documentation Required**: Dealer license, surety bond, business financial statements (2 years), floor plan agreement
- **Additional Benefits**: Wholesale pricing, auction access, priority allocation on high-demand models, 60-day floor plan terms

## APR Tiers

Financing interest rates are determined by the customer's credit score at the time of application. Velocity Motors offers the following **APR tiers**:

| Tier | Credit Score Range | APR | Typical Monthly Payment per $10,000 (60 mo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Excellent** | 750 and above | **3.9%** | $184 |
| **Good** | 700 - 749 | **5.4%** | $191 |
| **Fair** | 650 - 699 | **7.9%** | $202 |
| **Subprime** | 580 - 649 | **11.9%** | $222 |

APR is fixed for the life of the loan. Variable-rate financing is not offered. Rates shown are for New vehicles; CPO vehicles carry a 0.5% premium, and Used vehicles carry a 1.0% premium above the listed rates.

## Loan Terms

Velocity Motors offers the following **loan term options** for financed purchases:

| Loan Term | Available For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **24 months** | New, CPO, Used | Lowest total interest cost; highest monthly payment |
| **36 months** | New, CPO, Used | Popular for CPO and Used vehicles |
| **48 months** | New, CPO, Used | Balanced payment and total cost |
| **60 months** | New, CPO | Most popular term for New vehicles |
| **72 months** | New only | Available for Excellent and Good credit tiers only; requires minimum 15% down payment |

### Loan Term Restrictions

- **Used vehicles**: Maximum 48-month term, maximum loan-to-value ratio of 120%
- **CPO vehicles**: Maximum 60-month term, maximum loan-to-value ratio of 130%
- **New vehicles**: All terms available, maximum loan-to-value ratio of 140% (including tax, title, fees, and add-on products)
- **72-month loans**: Restricted to Excellent (750+) and Good (700-749) credit tiers. Minimum down payment of 15%.

## Lease Terms

Leasing is available for **New vehicles only** through manufacturer leasing programs. The following options are offered:

### Lease Duration

| Lease Term | Monthly Payment Factor | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **24 months** | Highest monthly, lowest total cost | Popular for luxury makes (BMW, Mercedes-Benz) |
| **36 months** | Most popular term | Best balance of payment and flexibility |
| **48 months** | Lowest monthly payment | Higher total cost; limited availability |

### Annual Mileage Allowance

| Mileage Tier | Annual Miles | Excess Mileage Charge |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** | 10,000 miles/year | $0.25/mile over allowance |
| **Standard** | 12,000 miles/year | $0.20/mile over allowance |
| **High** | 15,000 miles/year | $0.15/mile over allowance |

### Lease Financial Requirements

- **Due at Signing**: First month's payment + security deposit (equal to one monthly payment, rounded to nearest $50) + acquisition fee ($595-$895) + tax on down payment
- **Down Payment**: Optional but reduces monthly payment. Velocity Motors does not recommend large down payments on leases due to total-loss risk.
- **Disposition Fee**: $350-$595 charged at lease end if the vehicle is returned (waived if leasing or purchasing another Velocity Motors vehicle)

## Down Payment Requirements

### Financing Down Payment

- **Minimum**: 10% of the vehicle purchase price (before tax, title, and fees)
- **Recommended**: 20% to avoid negative equity and qualify for best rates
- **Sources**: Cash, check, trade-in equity, or combination
- **Negative Equity**: If the customer's trade-in has negative equity, the negative amount may be rolled into the new loan up to the maximum loan-to-value ratio for the vehicle condition category

### Lease Due at Signing

- **First month's payment**: Required
- **Security deposit**: Equal to one monthly payment rounded to nearest $50
- **Acquisition fee**: $595 - $895 depending on make
- **Additional down payment**: Optional, applied as a capitalized cost reduction

## Insurance Products

### Gap Insurance

**Gap Insurance** (Guaranteed Asset Protection) covers the difference between the vehicle's actual cash value and the outstanding loan or lease balance in the event of a total loss or theft.

- **Cost**: $400 - $800 (one-time premium, added to financing)
- **Eligibility**: Available for all financed and leased vehicles
- **Coverage Period**: Life of the loan or lease
- **Recommended For**: Customers with less than 20% down payment, customers financing for 60+ months, customers with negative equity trade-ins

Gap Insurance pricing by vehicle price bracket:

| Vehicle Price | Gap Insurance Cost |
|---|---|
| Under $30,000 | $400 - $500 |
| $30,000 - $60,000 | $500 - $650 |
| $60,000 - $100,000 | $600 - $750 |
| Over $100,000 | $700 - $800 |

### Extended Warranty

Extended Warranty plans are also offered as F&I products. For full plan details and pricing, see [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md):

- **3-Year Extended Warranty**: $1,500 - $2,500
- **5-Year Extended Warranty**: $2,500 - $4,000

Both Extended Warranty and Gap Insurance premiums may be included in the financed amount, spreading the cost over the loan term.

## Late Payment and Collections

### Payment Due Dates

- **Individual (SEG-001)**: Net 30 days from invoice date
- **Fleet (SEG-002)**: Net 45 days from invoice date
- **Dealer (SEG-003)**: Net 60 days from invoice date

### Late Payment Penalties

- **1-15 days late**: Courtesy reminder sent; no penalty
- **16-30 days late**: Late fee of 1.5% of outstanding balance
- **31-60 days late**: Late fee of 3% of outstanding balance; account placed on credit hold (no new purchases)
- **61-90 days late**: Account referred to collections; credit limit suspended
- **90+ days late**: Legal action may be initiated; account reported to credit bureaus

### Early Payoff

All Velocity Motors financing contracts allow early payoff with **no prepayment penalty**. Payoff amounts include principal and accrued interest through the payoff date. Customers may request a payoff quote online, by phone, or in person at any Velocity Motors location.
