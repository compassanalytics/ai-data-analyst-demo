# Velocity Motors — Service & Maintenance Policy

This document defines all service operations, pricing, scheduling standards, and customer satisfaction metrics for Velocity Motors service centers. Adherence to these service standards is mandatory for all technicians and service advisors. Maintenance compliance is required to maintain warranty coverage as described in [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md).

## Service Types and Pricing

Velocity Motors service centers perform **10 standard service types**. The table below summarizes each service with its cost range, duration, and share of total service volume:

| Service Type | Cost Range | Duration | Volume Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil Change | $30 - $80 | 30 - 60 min | 30% |
| Tire Rotation | $20 - $50 | 20 - 45 min | 15% |
| Brake Service | $150 - $800 | 60 - 180 min | 12% |
| Transmission Service | $200 - $1,500 | 120 - 480 min | 8% |
| Engine Repair | $500 - $5,000 | 240 - 960 min | 5% |
| AC Service | $100 - $500 | 60 - 180 min | 8% |
| Battery Replacement | $100 - $300 | 30 - 60 min | 7% |
| Alignment | $75 - $150 | 45 - 90 min | 5% |
| Inspection | $25 - $100 | 30 - 60 min | 8% |
| Recall Service | $0 | 60 - 240 min | 2% |

### Oil Change

**Cost**: $30 - $80 | **Duration**: 30 - 60 minutes | **Volume Share**: 30%

Oil changes are the highest-volume service, accounting for **30% of all service orders**. Pricing varies by oil type: conventional oil ($30-$45), synthetic blend ($45-$60), and full synthetic ($60-$80). All oil changes include a complimentary multi-point visual inspection and fluid top-off. Velocity Motors uses OEM-specified oil weights for all makes serviced.

### Tire Rotation

**Cost**: $20 - $50 | **Duration**: 20 - 45 minutes | **Volume Share**: 15%

Tire rotations account for **15% of service volume**. The service includes rotating tires according to the manufacturer-specified pattern, adjusting tire pressure to spec, and inspecting tread depth. Tires with tread depth below 3/32" are flagged for replacement recommendation.

### Brake Service

**Cost**: $150 - $800 | **Duration**: 60 - 180 minutes | **Volume Share**: 12%

Brake services represent **12% of volume** and range from pad replacement ($150-$300 per axle) to full brake system overhaul including rotors, calipers, and lines ($500-$800). Brake fluid flush is included with any rotor replacement. All brake components are sourced from OEM or OEM-equivalent suppliers.

### Transmission Service

**Cost**: $200 - $1,500 | **Duration**: 120 - 480 minutes | **Volume Share**: 8%

Transmission services are **8% of volume**. Basic fluid exchange runs $200-$400. Valve body repair or replacement ranges from $600-$1,200. Full transmission rebuild or replacement costs $1,000-$1,500 (parts and labor). CVT-equipped vehicles (common in Honda and Toyota models) require specialized fluid at a $50 premium.

### Engine Repair

**Cost**: $500 - $5,000 | **Duration**: 240 - 960 minutes | **Volume Share**: 5%

Engine repairs account for **5% of volume** but represent the highest per-ticket revenue. Minor repairs (sensors, gaskets, water pump) range from $500-$1,500. Major repairs (head gasket, timing chain, turbocharger) run $1,500-$3,500. Complete engine replacement ranges from $3,500-$5,000. BMW and Mercedes-Benz engine repairs typically run 20-40% higher than domestic makes.

### AC Service

**Cost**: $100 - $500 | **Duration**: 60 - 180 minutes | **Volume Share**: 8%

AC services are **8% of volume** with seasonal peaks in May through August. Basic recharge and leak check costs $100-$200. Compressor replacement runs $300-$500. All AC work uses R-1234yf refrigerant for 2017+ model years and R-134a for older vehicles.

### Battery Replacement

**Cost**: $100 - $300 | **Duration**: 30 - 60 minutes | **Volume Share**: 7%

Battery replacements are **7% of volume**. Standard lead-acid batteries cost $100-$175. AGM batteries (required for BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and vehicles with start-stop systems) cost $200-$300. Battery replacement includes terminal cleaning and charging system test.

### Alignment

**Cost**: $75 - $150 | **Duration**: 45 - 90 minutes | **Volume Share**: 5%

Wheel alignment services account for **5% of volume**. Two-wheel (front) alignment costs $75-$100. Four-wheel alignment costs $100-$150. Alignment is recommended after any suspension component replacement, tire replacement, or if the vehicle pulls to one side.

### Inspection

**Cost**: $25 - $100 | **Duration**: 30 - 60 minutes | **Volume Share**: 8%

Inspections represent **8% of service volume**. State safety inspections cost $25-$50. Comprehensive multi-point inspections cost $50-$100. Pre-purchase inspections for used vehicles cost $75-$100. All CPO certifications require a 150-point inspection (see [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md) for CPO details).

### Recall Service

**Cost**: $0 | **Duration**: 60 - 240 minutes | **Volume Share**: 2%

Recall services are **2% of volume** and are always performed at **$0 cost to the customer**. All costs are reimbursed by the manufacturer. Open recalls must be resolved before customer delivery of any vehicle. For recall procedures, NHTSA compliance, and safety feature details, see [Safety & Compliance Procedures](safety-compliance.md).

## Recommended Maintenance Intervals

Velocity Motors follows manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedules. The standard intervals below apply unless the vehicle's owner manual specifies otherwise:

| Maintenance Item | Interval | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oil Change | Every **5,000 miles** or 6 months | Synthetic oil may extend to 7,500 miles with manufacturer approval |
| Tire Rotation | Every **7,500 miles** | Perform with every other oil change |
| Brake Inspection | Every **15,000 miles** | Included in major service |
| Cabin & Engine Air Filter | Every **15,000 - 30,000 miles** | Varies by driving conditions |
| Transmission Fluid | Every **30,000 miles** | CVT fluid every 30,000; conventional every 60,000 |
| Major Service | Every **30,000 miles** | Full fluid exchange, multi-point inspection, spark plugs (if due) |
| Coolant Flush | Every **30,000 miles** | Or every 2 years, whichever comes first |
| Brake Fluid Flush | Every **30,000 miles** | Or every 2 years |
| Battery Test | Every **12 months** | Complimentary with any service visit |

**Failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule may void warranty coverage** for related components. See [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md) for specific warranty maintenance requirements.

## Fleet Customer Service Terms

Fleet customers (customer segment SEG-002) represent **20% of the Velocity Motors customer base** and receive specialized service terms.

### Fleet Service Requirements

- **Quarterly Inspections**: All fleet vehicles must undergo a multi-point inspection every 90 days regardless of mileage. Inspection cost: $50 per vehicle.
- **Priority Scheduling**: Fleet vehicles receive same-day or next-business-day scheduling for all routine maintenance. Emergency repairs are prioritized within 4 hours during business hours.
- **Dedicated Service Advisor**: Each fleet account is assigned a dedicated service advisor who manages scheduling, reporting, and cost tracking.

### Fleet Payment Terms

Fleet customers receive extended payment terms of **45 days** from invoice date (compared to 30 days for individual customers). Monthly service summaries are provided by the 5th business day of the following month. Volume discounts apply for fleets with 10+ vehicles: 10% discount on parts, 5% discount on labor.

### Fleet Reporting

Fleet managers receive monthly reports including:

- Total service spend by vehicle and category
- Upcoming scheduled maintenance by vehicle
- Out-of-compliance vehicles (overdue maintenance)
- Cost-per-mile analysis by vehicle age and make

## Customer Satisfaction Ratings

Velocity Motors collects customer satisfaction ratings for all completed service orders. The current rating distribution (based on **60% of completed orders** receiving a rating) is as follows:

| Rating | Stars | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | 5 stars | **50%** |
| Good | 4 stars | **30%** |
| Average | 3 stars | **12%** |
| Below Average | 2 stars | **5%** |
| Poor | 1 star | **3%** |

### Rating Collection Process

Surveys are sent via email within 24 hours of service completion. Customers rate their experience on a **5-star scale**. The **60% response rate** is measured on a rolling 12-month basis. Service advisors whose average rating falls below 3.5 stars for two consecutive months enter a performance improvement plan.

### Rating Benchmarks by Service Type

High-complexity services (engine repair, transmission service) typically receive lower average ratings (4.0-4.2 stars) than routine services (oil change, tire rotation) which average 4.5-4.7 stars. Recall services average 4.3 stars, reflecting customer satisfaction with zero-cost repairs offset by inconvenience.

## Service Scheduling and Capacity

### Operating Hours

- **Monday - Friday**: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- **Saturday**: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM
- **Sunday**: Closed (emergency fleet service available by appointment)

### Capacity Planning

Each Velocity Motors service center maintains capacity for the following daily targets:

- **Oil Changes**: 20-30 per day
- **Tire Rotations**: 12-18 per day
- **Brake Services**: 4-8 per day
- **Major Repairs** (engine, transmission): 2-4 per day
- **Inspections**: 8-12 per day

Peak service periods are January (post-holiday catch-up), April-May (pre-summer travel), and October-November (winterization). Service centers increase Saturday hours during peak periods.

## Parts Sourcing and Quality

All replacement parts must meet one of the following standards:

- **OEM Parts**: Manufacturer-original parts, required for all warranty and recall work
- **OEM-Equivalent Parts**: Third-party parts meeting or exceeding OEM specifications, acceptable for non-warranty service at customer request (10-25% cost savings)
- **Remanufactured Parts**: Factory-remanufactured components (transmissions, engines, alternators), offered with a 12-month warranty

Parts availability targets: **95%** of routine maintenance parts in stock, **85%** of common repair parts in stock, special-order parts within 2 business days. BMW and Mercedes-Benz parts may require 3-5 business days for less common components.

## Service Guarantees

Velocity Motors stands behind all service work with the following guarantees:

- **12-Month / 12,000-Mile Warranty**: All parts and labor for repairs performed at Velocity Motors service centers
- **Price Match**: Velocity Motors will match any written estimate from a licensed repair facility within 25 miles for the same service using equivalent parts
- **On-Time Promise**: If a scheduled service exceeds the quoted time by more than 30 minutes, the customer receives a 10% discount on labor

Effective Date: January 2026
