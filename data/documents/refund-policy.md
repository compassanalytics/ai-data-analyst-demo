# Refund Policy

## Overview

Our company is committed to customer satisfaction. We offer a generous refund policy that allows customers to request refunds under various circumstances. This policy applies to all products and services unless otherwise specified in your contract.

## 30-Day Full Refund Window

All customers are entitled to a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. This applies to:

- Monthly subscription plans
- Annual subscription plans (prorated refund available after 30 days)
- One-time purchases
- Add-on services

To request a refund during this period, simply submit a request through your account dashboard or contact our support team.

## Partial Refund (30-60 Days)

After the initial 30-day window, customers may still request a partial refund:

- **Days 31-45**: 75% refund available
- **Days 46-60**: 50% refund available
- **After 60 days**: Refunds evaluated on a case-by-case basis

Partial refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and credited to the original payment method.

## Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers with custom contracts may have different refund terms as specified in their service agreement. Key differences may include:

- Extended refund windows (up to 90 days)
- Custom proration schedules
- Dedicated account manager for refund processing
- Transfer credits to other services instead of monetary refund

Please refer to your enterprise agreement or contact your account manager for specific terms.

## Refund Process

1. **Submit Request**: Log into your account and navigate to Billing > Request Refund, or email support@company.com
2. **Verification**: Our team will verify your purchase and eligibility within 24 hours
3. **Approval**: Once approved, you'll receive a confirmation email
4. **Processing**: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
5. **Confirmation**: You'll receive a final confirmation when the refund is complete

## Exceptions

The following are not eligible for refunds:

- Services already fully consumed or delivered
- Custom development work that has been completed
- Third-party integrations purchased through our marketplace
- Promotional or heavily discounted purchases (unless defective)
