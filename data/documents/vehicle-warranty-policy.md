# Velocity Motors — Vehicle Warranty Policy

This document defines the warranty coverage, terms, and conditions for all vehicles sold through Velocity Motors dealerships. All warranty claims are subject to the terms outlined herein and require compliance with the maintenance schedules described in [Service & Maintenance Policy](service-maintenance-policy.md).

## Standard Warranty Coverage

Every **New** vehicle sold by Velocity Motors includes the following factory-backed warranty coverage at no additional cost:

- **Bumper-to-Bumper Warranty**: 3 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. Covers all vehicle components except normal wear items (brake pads, wiper blades, tires, filters).
- **Powertrain Warranty**: 5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first. Covers engine, transmission, transfer case, drive axles, and all internally lubricated components.
- **Corrosion Warranty**: 5 years, unlimited mileage. Covers perforation from corrosion (rust-through) on body panels.
- **Emissions Warranty**: 8 years or 80,000 miles on major emissions components as required by federal law.

Coverage begins on the date of original retail delivery or the date the vehicle is first placed into service, whichever is earlier. Warranty repairs must be performed at an authorized Velocity Motors service center.

## Vehicle Condition Categories

Velocity Motors classifies all inventory into three condition categories, each with distinct warranty provisions:

### New Vehicles (60% of Inventory)

New vehicles represent **60%** of Velocity Motors inventory. These vehicles carry full factory warranty coverage as described under Standard Warranty Coverage above. New vehicles are eligible for all Extended Warranty plans at point of sale.

### Certified Pre-Owned Vehicles (25% of Inventory)

**Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)** vehicles account for **25%** of inventory. Every CPO vehicle undergoes a rigorous **150-point inspection** covering:

| Inspection Area | Points Checked |
|---|---|
| Engine & Drivetrain | 32 points |
| Electrical Systems | 24 points |
| Brakes & Suspension | 22 points |
| Body & Interior | 28 points |
| Safety Features | 20 points |
| Road Test Performance | 24 points |

CPO vehicles receive an **additional 1 year or 12,000 miles** of warranty coverage beyond any remaining factory warranty. If the original factory bumper-to-bumper warranty has expired, the CPO warranty covers powertrain and major systems for 1 year or 12,000 miles from the CPO purchase date.

### Used Vehicles (15% of Inventory)

Used vehicles make up **15%** of inventory. These vehicles are sold with a **limited 30-day or 1,000-mile powertrain warranty**, whichever comes first. This limited warranty covers:

- Engine block and internal components
- Transmission and torque converter
- Drive axle assemblies

Used vehicle buyers are strongly encouraged to purchase an Extended Warranty plan at the time of sale. The limited 30-day warranty does **not** cover electrical systems, air conditioning, suspension, or body components.

## Warranty Maintenance Requirements

**All warranty coverage — standard and extended — requires the vehicle owner to follow the manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedule.** Failure to perform required maintenance may void warranty coverage for related components.

### Required Maintenance for Warranty Validity

The following maintenance items must be performed at the specified intervals to maintain warranty coverage:

- **Oil Change**: Every **5,000 miles** or 6 months (see [Service & Maintenance Policy](service-maintenance-policy.md) for costs: $30-$80)
- **Tire Rotation**: Every **7,500 miles** or with every other oil change
- **Major Service**: Every **30,000 miles** — includes transmission fluid, coolant, brake fluid, and multi-point inspection
- **Annual Inspection**: Required annually regardless of mileage

Owners must retain all service records. Velocity Motors service centers automatically log maintenance history in the dealership management system. Outside maintenance is accepted with valid receipts from licensed repair facilities.

## Warranty Claims Process

### Filing a Claim

1. **Schedule Service**: Contact any Velocity Motors service center or call the warranty hotline at 1-800-555-VMOT.
2. **Diagnosis**: A certified technician diagnoses the concern and determines warranty eligibility.
3. **Authorization**: Claims under $500 are authorized at the service center level. Claims over $500 require regional warranty administrator approval (typically within 24 hours).
4. **Repair**: Covered repairs are performed at no cost to the customer (subject to applicable deductible for Extended Warranty plans).
5. **Documentation**: The repair order is logged with the claim number, parts used, labor hours, and final cost.

### Claim Denial Reasons

Warranty claims may be denied for the following reasons:

- Vehicle has been modified with non-approved aftermarket parts that contributed to the failure
- Required maintenance was not performed per the maintenance schedule
- Damage caused by accident, misuse, neglect, or environmental factors (flood, hail)
- Odometer has been tampered with or is inoperable
- Vehicle was used for racing, commercial hauling beyond rated capacity, or off-road use (unless designed for such use)

### Recall-Related Claims

Open safety recalls are handled separately from warranty claims. Recall service is always performed at **$0 cost** to the customer regardless of warranty status. See [Safety & Compliance Procedures](safety-compliance.md) for recall handling requirements, NHTSA compliance, and pre-delivery inspection protocols.

## Warranty Transfer Rules

Velocity Motors warranty coverage is transferable to subsequent owners under the following terms:

| Warranty Type | Transfer Fee | Transferable |
|---|---|---|
| Bumper-to-Bumper (Standard) | **$0** | Yes — automatically transfers with vehicle sale |
| Powertrain (Standard) | **$0** | Yes — automatically transfers with vehicle sale |
| CPO Additional Coverage | **$0** | Yes — transfers once to next private-party buyer |
| 3-Year Extended Warranty | **$50** | Yes — must be requested within 30 days of ownership change |
| 5-Year Extended Warranty | **$50** | Yes — must be requested within 30 days of ownership change |

To transfer an Extended Warranty plan, the new owner must submit a transfer request with proof of purchase, the original warranty contract number, and the $50 transfer fee. The remaining coverage term and mileage limits are unchanged by the transfer.

## Warranty Exclusions

The following items are **not covered** under any Velocity Motors warranty:

- Normal wear and replacement items: brake pads, brake rotors (unless defective), clutch disc, wiper blades, light bulbs, fuses, filters, belts, hoses, tires
- Cosmetic damage: paint chips, dents, interior stains, upholstery wear
- Maintenance services: oil changes, fluid top-offs, wheel alignments, tire balancing
- Aftermarket accessories not installed by Velocity Motors
- Damage from using incorrect fuel type, oil, or fluids
- Glass breakage from road debris

## Customer Dispute Resolution

If a warranty claim is denied and the customer disputes the decision:

1. **Service Manager Review**: The service center manager reviews the denial within 3 business days.
2. **Regional Warranty Review**: If unresolved, the claim escalates to the regional warranty administrator for independent review within 7 business days.
3. **Manufacturer Arbitration**: For factory warranty disputes, the customer may request binding arbitration through the manufacturer's dispute resolution program.

For return and exchange rights related to warranty issues, see [Return & Exchange Policy](return-exchange-policy.md). Lemon law provisions are detailed in that document.
