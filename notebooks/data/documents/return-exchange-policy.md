# Velocity Motors — Return & Exchange Policy

This document defines the return, exchange, cancellation, and lemon law provisions for all vehicles sold through Velocity Motors dealerships. These policies ensure customer satisfaction while protecting the dealership from undue loss. For warranty-related defect claims, see [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md). For financing adjustments during exchanges, see [Financing & Payment Terms](financing-terms.md).

## Order Cancellation

Velocity Motors maintains a **5% order cancellation rate** across all vehicle purchases. Cancellation terms vary by timing and stage of the transaction.

### Pre-Delivery Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled at no cost before the vehicle has been delivered and before the customer takes physical possession. Pre-delivery cancellations include:

- **Custom/Factory Orders**: Cancellable with full deposit refund until the vehicle enters transit from the manufacturer. Once in transit, a $500 restocking fee applies.
- **In-Stock Vehicles with Deposit**: Fully refundable if cancelled within 48 hours of deposit. After 48 hours, the deposit is applied as a credit toward a future purchase valid for 12 months.

### Post-Delivery Cancellation

Once the customer has taken physical delivery, the order **cannot be cancelled**. The customer may pursue an exchange within the applicable exchange window (see below) or sell the vehicle back to Velocity Motors at fair market value.

## Exchange Policy

Velocity Motors offers a limited exchange window for customers who are not satisfied with their purchase. **No returns are permitted after title transfer** — exchanges are the sole remedy.

### Exchange Windows by Vehicle Condition

The exchange window varies based on the condition category of the purchased vehicle:

| Condition | Inventory Share | Exchange Window | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New** | 60% | **7 days or 500 miles** (whichever comes first) | Vehicle must be in same condition as delivery, no damage, all accessories included |
| **CPO** | 25% | **7 days or 500 miles** (whichever comes first) | Vehicle must pass CPO re-inspection at return; CPO inspection verification required |
| **Used** | 15% | **5 days or 500 miles** (whichever comes first) | Vehicle must be in same condition as delivery, powertrain functional |

### Exchange Process

1. **Notification**: Customer must notify their sales representative or the sales manager within the exchange window. Written or electronic notice is accepted.
2. **Vehicle Inspection**: The returning vehicle is inspected for damage, excessive wear, or mileage beyond the 500-mile limit. Any damage not present at delivery will be deducted from the exchange credit.
3. **Vehicle Selection**: The customer selects a replacement vehicle from current inventory. The replacement must be of equal or greater value. If greater value, the customer pays the difference.
4. **Financing Adjustment**: If the original purchase was financed, the financing contract is unwound and a new contract is executed for the replacement vehicle. See [Financing & Payment Terms](financing-terms.md) for payment method details.
5. **Documentation**: New purchase agreement, title application, and registration are processed. The original title application is voided.

### Exchange Restrictions

- Only **one exchange** is permitted per purchase
- The exchange must be completed within **3 business days** of the exchange request
- Vehicles with **more than 500 miles** added since delivery are not eligible
- Vehicles with **any damage** (collision, vandalism, weather) are not eligible for exchange
- **Fleet purchases** (segment SEG-002) and **Dealer purchases** (segment SEG-003) are not eligible for exchange — these are governed by separate commercial agreements

## Discount Tier Preservation

When a customer exercises an exchange, any discount applied to the original purchase is preserved on the replacement vehicle. Velocity Motors applies discounts across **4 tiers**:

| Discount Tier | Discount Range | Share of Sales |
|---|---|---|
| No Discount | 0% | **30%** |
| Small Discount | 1% - 5% | **50%** |
| Medium Discount | 5% - 10% | **15%** |
| Large Discount | 10% - 15% | **5%** |

### Discount Preservation Rules

- The **same percentage discount** (not dollar amount) is applied to the replacement vehicle's MSRP
- If the replacement vehicle is in a higher price bracket, the discount percentage still applies, resulting in a larger dollar discount
- Discounts originally negotiated as a dollar amount are converted to a percentage of the original MSRP for application to the replacement
- **Large discounts (10-15%)** require sales manager approval for preservation during exchange
- Loyalty and repeat-customer discounts are preserved automatically

## Lemon Law Provisions

Velocity Motors complies with all applicable state lemon law statutes. The following summary describes the general lemon law framework applicable to vehicles sold by Velocity Motors.

### Lemon Law Qualification Criteria

A vehicle may qualify for lemon law relief if it meets **both** of the following conditions:

1. **Same Substantial Defect**: The same substantial defect has been subject to repair **3 or more times** within the warranty period, and the defect has not been corrected.
2. **Cumulative Days Out of Service**: The vehicle has been out of service for a cumulative total of **30 or more days** due to warranty repairs during the warranty coverage period.

A "substantial defect" is defined as a condition that substantially impairs the use, value, or safety of the vehicle. It does **not** include defects caused by owner abuse, neglect, unauthorized modification, or failure to perform required maintenance.

### Lemon Law Process

1. **Documentation**: The customer must provide complete service records showing 3+ repair attempts for the same defect or 30+ days out of service. All repairs must have been performed at authorized service centers.
2. **Manufacturer Notification**: Velocity Motors assists the customer in formally notifying the manufacturer in writing, providing a final opportunity to repair the defect.
3. **Final Repair Attempt**: The manufacturer is given one additional repair opportunity (typically 10-14 business days).
4. **Resolution**: If the defect persists after the final repair attempt, the customer may elect either:
   - **Replacement**: A comparable new vehicle of the same make, model, and equipment level
   - **Repurchase**: Full refund of the purchase price, including taxes, fees, and financing charges, minus a reasonable mileage offset

### Lemon Law and Warranty Interaction

Lemon law claims are processed through the vehicle manufacturer, not directly through Velocity Motors. However, Velocity Motors service records serve as the primary documentation for lemon law claims. All warranty repair orders include detailed technician notes, parts replaced, and days the vehicle was in the service center's possession. For warranty coverage details, see [Vehicle Warranty Policy](vehicle-warranty-policy.md).

## Refund Processing

### Timeline

- **Deposit Refunds**: Processed within 5 business days to the original payment method
- **Exchange Price Differences**: Collected or refunded at the time of the exchange transaction
- **Lemon Law Repurchases**: Processed within 30 days of manufacturer approval
- **Cancelled Financing**: Lender payoff processed within 10 business days; any equity refunded to customer within 15 business days

### Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method whenever possible. Cash purchases are refunded by check. Financed purchases require lender coordination — the loan is paid off first, and any remaining equity is refunded to the customer by check.

## Title and Registration

### Title Transfer Timing

Title transfer is initiated within **3 business days** of delivery. Once the title has been transferred to the customer's name at the state DMV, the vehicle is considered sold and no return is possible. Exchanges within the exchange window halt the title transfer process if it has not yet been completed.

### Registration During Exchange

If the customer exercises an exchange after registration has been completed on the original vehicle, Velocity Motors covers the cost of re-registration for the replacement vehicle. The customer is not charged duplicate registration fees or taxes on the replacement (taxes are adjusted to reflect only the price difference, if any).

## Special Situations

### Vehicles Sold with Known Defects

If a vehicle is sold with a known and disclosed defect (documented on the purchase agreement), that specific defect is excluded from exchange and lemon law eligibility. All other defects remain covered.

### Trade-In Vehicles

If the original purchase included a trade-in vehicle and the customer exercises an exchange, the trade-in value applied to the original deal is preserved. If the trade-in vehicle has already been resold by Velocity Motors, the trade-in credit is applied as a dollar amount toward the replacement vehicle.

### Aftermarket Additions

Any aftermarket accessories or modifications added after delivery are the customer's responsibility. Velocity Motors is not obligated to install aftermarket items from the original vehicle onto the replacement vehicle. Refunds are not issued for aftermarket work performed by third parties.
